# Space and mounting

Reserve space for the board, its tallest components, both connectors and the antenna installation—not just the board outline.

Rev A design values: **80 × 60 × 1.6 mm** board, 5 mm corner radii, four M3 mounting holes (3.2 mm diameter) on a **72 × 52 mm** pattern. Supercapacitors extend about **22 mm above the board**; this is not a guaranteed assembled-height measurement.

A dimensioned customer drawing, verified height, mass and customer 3D model remain pending. Environmental targets are not validated limits.
